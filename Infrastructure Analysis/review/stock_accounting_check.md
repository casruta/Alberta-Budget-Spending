# Independent stock-accounting check

Read 2024-25 Final Results Year-End Report, PDF/printed pages 11, 12 and 14.

The historical table row labelled Capital / non-fin. Assets reports $58,845 million in 2024-25, whereas the balance sheet gross capital / other non-financial assets is $62,925 million. The difference equals spent deferred capital contributions of $4,080 million: 62,925 − 4,080 = 58,845. For 2023-24, 61,515 − 3,964 = 57,551, matching the historical series.

Do not call historical-table row 30 a clean tangible-infrastructure stock series. It includes other nonfinancial assets and appears net of deferred capital contributions. Page 11 separately says capital assets are $61.7 billion in March 2025, an increase of $1.3 billion, comprising $3.9 billion capital investment less $2.6 billion amortization, disposals and accounting adjustments. This does not equal the Capital Plan $4.3 billion investment component, whose consolidation and accounting treatment requires further documentation.

Capital Plan includes grants to entities outside the provincial consolidation perimeter; municipal assets do not automatically appear in the provincial balance sheet. Values at depreciated accounting cost do not establish lane kilometres, usable beds, building quality or replacement cost. Report this as a supplemental accounting observation rather than a physical growth measure.
