## Political timing and peak

2018–19 is the last complete fiscal year beforeKenney's30 April2019 accession.2019–20 is not a pure policy-exposure observation: one month precedes accession, legacy contracts and cashflow schedules continue, and the government budget was introduced later in that fiscal year. The current narrative appropriately labels government tenure and avoids deriving policy attribution from month counts. If policy timing is analyzed later, accession, budget enactment, appropriation and project approval need separate dates; do not simply label every2019 dollar UCP-policy treatment.

2017–18's9021M capital peak includes accelerated municipal support, including documented800M MSI reprofiled from future years. The current spike sensitivity excludes the entire year rather than merely the800M. It is accurately called coarse; it does not restore later-year grants or generate a normalized infrastructure delivery path. Broad pre/post averages remain descriptive, not causal.

## Population and inflation

July1 estimates are three months after fiscal-year start, not fiscal-year midpoint. The report now identifies fiscal-start calendar-year population and CPI consistently; the rounding scenarios do not cure calendar/fiscal misalignment. Compounding starts after the chosen baseline year, which remains correct.

The2019–20→2024–25 CPI-adjusted change−1.4% is materially smaller than unquantified timing, revision and price-measure uncertainty. Current “approximately flat” wording explicitly invokes those unresolved uncertainties; it is not a claim of statistical equivalence. The2018–19→2024–25−12.6% headline remains true for its disclosed CPI/July inputs, not established robustness against every possible appropriate construction deflator.

The new price-sensitivity curve is a deterministic counterfactual, with no probability model or confidence interval. It shows the5.003% break-even price factor but is not evidence construction prices increased beyond that level. The draft does not infer actual construction volume from it.
