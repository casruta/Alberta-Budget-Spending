# Correction and definition check

The original health findings merged Grande Prairie phases into a single 2021–22 completion. Corrected against printed page 23 of both official reports: Phase 1 is listed completed in 2020–21, and Phase 2 in 2021–22. The $850 million label covers both phases jointly and cannot be added across rows.

Both 2020–21 and 2021–22 define completed as “operational,” with potential remaining closeout spending. The 2022–23 and subsequent reports define completed as construction ended, potentially with preoperational spending. Consequently completion categories are not a uniform service-opening measure across years. Do not infer an exact whole-hospital patient-opening date from the phase labels.

The 2024–25 annual report printed page 15 explicitly states three communities are open in Red Deer, Lethbridge and Gunn, providing 200 treatment beds. Page 20 separately classifies the same stock as existing. CSV and narrative now cite both. Original source excerpts are preserved.
