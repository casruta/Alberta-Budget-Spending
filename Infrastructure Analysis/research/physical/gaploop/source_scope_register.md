# Source coverage and comparison limits after the deeper pass

## Searches completed

Searched all selected 2015–25 mechanically extracted PDFs for student/school/learning spaces; new/modernized/replaced school completions; enrolment; continuing-care/long-term-care spaces; beds/dialysis/operating rooms; housing units; courtrooms; road rehabilitation/twinning/route kilometres; pavement/facility/bridge condition; water treatment/quality/operational assets; digital centres/broadband; electricity capacity; municipal infrastructure; and completed/operational P3 s. Older long reports include Measuring Up sections and methodological notes; newer short reports focus on consolidated financials and capital-plan select projects. Source absence does not imply provincial data absence.

## Reporting availability is uneven

- **2015–16/2016–17/2017–18:** full annual reports expose service indicators and methods alongside financials. Useful additions include pupil spaces, mixed school-project completions, continuing-care access and government-building energy intensity.
- **2018–19 to 2024–25:** shorter year-end consolidated reports contain financial capital inputs, selected named projects, some gross delivery indicators and rounded stock snapshots. They do not replace institutional/departmental/municipal asset inventories.
- **Physical maps:**2021–22 p. 19 is an image; text extraction missed its project names. Visual inspection independently verified Southwest Calgary Ring Road, Bragg Creek Flood Mitigation, Northern Lights Gas Line, Quest, High Prairie Renal Dialysis and Grande Prairie Phase 2 on completed side, while West Calgary Ring Road and Southwest Anthony Henday Expansion remain on ongoing side. Absence from extracted text is not absence from a PDF.

## Demand/staffing observations versus infrastructure

2015–16 report says 2015–16 school-enrolment growth reached 2.7% and 740 more teachers were added to classrooms (physical PDF p. 14).2017–18 report says higher-than-expected enrolment required 830 more classroomstaff (physical PDF p. 18). These are demand/staffing observations, not physical seats; “teachers” and “classroomstaff” are different units. There is no standardized endpoint enrolment count in the newer short consolidated sources to build a decade-long seats-per-pupil ratio.

The 2017–18 post-secondary participation indicator(physical PDF p. 129) explicitly warns its sample-survey estimate is **not a true enrolment-to-population ratio**. It is unsuitable as a campus-capacity denominator or substitute for administrative student counts. Electricity generation metrics also include privatelyowned facilities outside the provincial capital-budget asset boundary; avoid inserting them into a public-budget stock numerator without a distinct scope.

## No defensible imputation bridges

Do not fill missing seats from an assumed seats-per-school mean, beds from construction funding, bridge numbers from projects completed, route km from rehabilitation project counts, water connections from grants, or broadband connections from strategy expenditures. Do not infer condition from maintenance dollars, convert gross replacement capacity to net addition without retired assets, or map first observed completed-status year to actual handover year without an explicit dated source.

## Recommended interpretation of new baseline evidence

A figure can show discrete source observations with direct measure labels:2017–18 approx 21600 new spaces, 2021–22 more than 5000 new spaces, 2024–25 more than 11000 new-and-modernizedspaces. It must show differing scope/precision and cannot connect them as a homogeneous growth time series. An approximate point estimate, lower bound, and combined gross measure are not three comparable net capacity observations.

## Outstanding external data requirements

Schoolnet seats and enrolment require Alberta Education/institutionadministrative inventories; hospitalcapacity requires comparable staffed/licensedbed data and AHS/CIHI source notes; transport needs route/lane inventories and dated opening records; water/wastewater requires municipal plant capacities/service connections; housing requires eligibility/ownership/retirement reconciliation; broadband requires verified premisesconnected. Funding remains measured separately. Source blocked access was not retried without a changed diagnosis, and no undisclosed external factual assumptions were introduced.
