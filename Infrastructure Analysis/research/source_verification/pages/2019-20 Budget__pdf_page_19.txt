Construction in Progress 
Continued
Motor Vehicles System 
(MOVES) Upgrade
Total Project Cost: $22.1 million 
Expected Completion: Spring 2021
•	 A major upgrade to the province’s 
motor vehicle system, MOVES, 
is underway. MOVES is a critical 
system used by Service Alberta and 
Alberta Transportation that also 
interfaces to systems used in many 
other departments.
Completed Projects
Highway 2/Gaetz 
Avenue Interchange
Total Project Cost: $80 million
•	 A multi-year project to construct a 
new interchange south of Red Deer 
at Highway 2, Gaetz Avenue and 
Taylor Drive.
Highway 43X - Grande 
Prairie Bypass
Total Project Cost: $48 million 
•	 This project involved construction of 
12 km on a four-lane highway and 
a roundabout at Highway 43 and 
Range Road 63 – the first one built 
on a divided highway in Alberta.
Keyano College, 
Campus Upgrades
Total Project Cost: $15.6 million  
•	 This project addressed deterioration 
and safety issues impacting campus 
facilities.
University of Lethbridge  
Destination Project
Total Project Cost: $247.7 million  
•	 Completed January 2020, this 
project involved replacing the 
outdated and over-crowded science 
building on campus. 
Red Deer Secure 
Detoxification and Residential 
Treatment Facility
Total Project Cost: $9.1 million  
•	 Creation of an eight-bed program 
to provide comprehensive 
assessment, detoxification, addictions 
counselling, health services, and 24-
hour care.
Flood Recovery - Provincial 
Park Infrastructure Restoration 
Total Project Cost: $15.3 million
•	 These funds addressed flood damage 
from the 2013 flooding in Southern 
Alberta.
