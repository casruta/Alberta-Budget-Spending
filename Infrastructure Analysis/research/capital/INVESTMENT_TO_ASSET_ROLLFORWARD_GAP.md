## Relevant source definitions and attempted bridges

Original p16 defines Capital Investment as “cash spent on acquisition of Government of Alberta capital assets including hospitals,schools,and provincial highways,” recorded as capital assets on the Statement of Financial Position. It separately defines capital grants to outside-government entities,recorded as expenses. Therefore referring to4,309M as the source's **capital-plan investment in provincially consolidated assets** is consistent with the quoted source definition. That wording does not establish identity to every stock-rollforward component.

Original p9 explicitly describes cash adjustments for accrued revenue versus cash,noncash revenue/expense,SUCH/entity results,and transfers. Original p10 presents an investment **cash requirement excluding SUCH self-financed** of3,437M. This is not the3.9B figure and not the full4,309M. Adding744M SUCH gives4,181M,which does not reconcile to4,309M. This arithmetic demonstrates that these labeled subscopes should not be naively forced together; it does **not identify the remaining128M as a named adjustment**.

Original p18 says higher SUCH self-financed investment reflects post-secondary capital projects and donated assets. That establishes that donations affect reported investment/support,not that donations explain the entire investment-versus-stock narrative difference. No amount explicitly bridges donations to3.9B.

Original p12 lists inventory,prepayments and purchased intangibles,and their changes(−19M inventory acquisition-versus-consumption/write-downs,+84M prepaids,+8M purchased intangibles). Their stocks or changes cannot legitimately be subtracted from4,309M as a complete reconciliation absent source mapping. P3 principal repayments74M and alternative financing4M likewise concern financing,not automatically missing capital-asset investment.

Original p12 points to **Schedule17,p81 of the2024–25 Consolidated Financial Statements** for accounting-standard adjustments. The full financial-statement schedules/tangible-capital rollforward are not supplied within the local24page Final Results excerpt. These detailed statements—and the underlying Capital Plan reconciliation definitions—are the required next sources to bridge asset additions,construction in progress,noncash/donated assets,scope adjustments and financing correctly. Their contents cannot be inferred from the short year-end summary.
