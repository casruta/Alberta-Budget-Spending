REVENUE PREDICTION ANALYSIS: KEY FINDINGS
==========================================
Prepared: 2026-02-26
Numerical corrections reviewed: 2026-10-11; original preparation date retained.
Source: Alberta Fiscal Plan 2026-2029

1. REVENUE TRAJECTORY
   - Total revenue drops from $82.5B (2024-25 actual) to $74.6B (2026-27 estimate), a 9.6% decline.
   - Partial recovery to $81.5B by 2028-29, but still $1.0B below 2024-25 levels.
   - Year-over-year: -8.7% (2025-26), -1.0% (2026-27), +5.9% (2027-28), +3.3% (2028-29).

2. RESOURCE REVENUE COLLAPSE
   - Bitumen royalty falls from $17.2B to $9.7B (-43.6%), the single largest revenue shock.
   - Other resource revenue declines from $4.8B to $3.5B (-26.9%).
   - Total resource revenue: $22.0B -> $13.2B at trough, recovering to $16.9B by 2028-29.
   - Resource revenue share drops from 26.7% to 17.7% of total revenue.

3. TAX REVENUE (PARTIAL OFFSET)
   - Tax revenue is $30.356B actual in 2024-25 and $30.470B estimated in 2026-27 (+0.4%).
   - The $33.177B (+9.3%) value is the 2028-29 target, not the 2026-27 estimate.
   - Growth to 2026-27: education property tax (+28.4%), insurance tax (+16.0%), tourism levy (+58.7%).
   - Decline to 2026-27: corporate income tax (-10.2%), tobacco/vaping tax (-7.0%).
   - Tax revenue share rises from 36.8% actual to 40.9% in 2026-27, reflecting a composition shift.

4. FEDERAL TRANSFERS
   - 2024-25 actual $12.618B -> 2026-27 estimate $13.715B (+8.7%); share rises 15.3% -> 18.4%.
   - 2028-29 target $13.839B is 9.7% above 2024-25; its revenue share is 17.0%.

5. FISCAL GAP
   - Alberta moves from $8.3B surplus (2024-25) to deficits of $4.1B to $9.4B.
   - Cumulative deficit 2025-26 to 2028-29: approximately $28.0B.
   - Worst year: 2026-27 at -$9.4B.
   - The gap is driven by revenue declining while expenses grow 7.1%, 5.7%, 3.1%, 2.2% annually.

6. STRUCTURAL RISK
   - The plan projects expense above revenue through its forecast window.
   - These projections do not isolate the structural or cyclical share of the deficit.
   - Alberta's revenue base remains highly exposed to commodity price volatility.
   - The plan projects deficits through 2028-29; effects of alternative policies are not estimated here.
