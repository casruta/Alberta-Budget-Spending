Table Corrections

  • Correction date: 2020-02-24
    • On February 24, 2020, estimates of Quebec and Canada after-tax income for 2017 were corrected, leading to a revison of the low income measure (LIM) after-tax thresholds for 2017. As a result, LIM estimates were revised for all geographies, while Market Basket Measure (MBM) and after-tax low income cut-off (LICO) estimates were revised for Quebec, Canada and Quebec census metropolitan areas.
