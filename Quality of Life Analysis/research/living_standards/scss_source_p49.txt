Description: Career and Employment Information Services (CEIS) help Albertans to understand 
the labour market, make informed career decisions, develop realistic education, training and 
employment plans and to prepare for, find and maintain work. This performance measure captures 
the proportion (per cent) of former CEIS clients who found employment within three months after 
completing one of the following CEIS services: Exposure Course, Job Placement, Workshop, and 
Disability Related Employment Supports (DRES). 
Rationale: This performance measure assesses the impact of CEIS on employability of CEIS clients. 
The measure includes the following services: Workshops, Exposure Course, Job Placement, DRES, 
and Workforce Development training. They do not include Assessments or other service types not 
surveyed via Work Outcomes Reporting Project. 
Results and variance explanation: In 2024, 67 per cent of participants reported employment 
three months after receiving CEIS. This is a decrease of one percentage point from the 2023 result 
of 68 per cent and 2 percentage points above the 2024 target. In 2024, 75 per cent of Albertans 
reported they were satisfied or very satisfied with the CEIS services they received. 
The variance between percentage of participants employed after receiving employment services in 
2024 from 2023 may be explained by continuing changes in Alberta’s labour market. Alberta’s 
unemployment rate increased from an average of 5.8 per cent in 2023 to 7.1 per cent in 2024, due 
in part to rapid population growth that outpaced job creation. Alberta’s job vacancy rates reached 
3.1 per cent as of November 2024, a reduction from 2022 highs, representing a substantial decrease 
in job opportunities. Under these weak market conditions, the 2024 result of 67 per cent may 
reflect the utility of employment services in helping individuals navigate a tighter labour market. 
Those who participated in employment services without obtaining employment may have 
transitioned to further education or training, obtained employment that was not captured in 
reporting, moved out of Alberta or received financial support from other sources
