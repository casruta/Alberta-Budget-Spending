Note: 
This measure was introduced in the 2022-25 Seniors and Housing Business Plan. Due to variation in data collection and processing, comparable 
historical results are not available prior to the 2021-22 period.  
Description: This measure reports on the number of new affordable housing units and new rental 
subsidies the ministry has funded or supported for Albertans in need within a fiscal year. The 
results of this measure include newly built affordable housing units across five capital programs 
and additional households supported under the Rent Supplement program (including the Housing 
Benefit for Survivors of Gender-Based Violence, which launched in 2024-25). 
Rationale: This measure is a direct reflection of progress made toward achieving the goal of 
supporting 25,000 more households by 2032-33 set out in Stronger Foundations: Alberta’s 10-year 
strategy to improve and expand affordable housing.  
Results and variance explanation: In 2024-25, the ministry delivered 798 new affordable housing 
units and new rental subsidies to Albertans with low income.  The result reflects the timing and 
status of capital builds as most of the capital projects are currently under construction and in 
progress that will be coming online in future years. As of March 31, 2025, 1,626 units and 359 
shelters spaces are in progress. In addition, the results reflect rising rent prices and corresponding 
increases in the maximum benefit available have led to higher individual benefits. As a result, more 
funds are allocated to ensure households already in the program are able to maintain their housing, 
leaving fewer resources available to expand to new households. The government has increased the 
funding for both affordable housing capital projects and rent assistance moving forward.
