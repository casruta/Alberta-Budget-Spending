# Continuing-care outcomes after ministry transfer

The official SCSS 2024–25 report **does report** the transferred timeliness outcome as Performance Measure 4a, physical PDF page 64 (printed 62): 61.3% in 2020–21, 65.6% in 2021–22, 64.1% in 2022–23, 62.5% in 2023–24, and 63.0% in 2024–25. The 2024–25 target is 66.0%, so the preliminary actual is 3.0 percentage points below target. It is 0.5 percentage point higher than the reported 2023–24 value, but **the 2023–24 provincial data is incomplete**: since May 2023 some zones could not report due to Connect Care implementation. The report explicitly calls 2024–25 preliminary. Accordingly the observed increase cannot establish a complete-population improvement.

The methodology on physical PDF page 71 (printed 69) defines the total percentage as clients moved to a continuing-care home within 30 days of assessed-and-approved date divided by **all clients admitted to such homes during the reporting period**. It includes types A (long-term care) and B (designated supportive living) and excludes type C community hospices. Assessed-and-approved date is waitlist entry following assessment/approval. This closely matches the earlier admitted-client timeliness concept; there is no statistical bridge needed to the **14 largest hospital waitlist count**, because that is a different metric and never should have replaced/spliced into the timeliness percentage. Both can coexist in separate evidence tables.

The CSV now records all five percentages with SCSS 2024–25 vintage in notes, retaining the earlier vintages for their nonoverlapping years. No claim is made that historical SCSS values are unrevised: no explicit revision footnote is present on the inspected table. The missing 2019–20 timeliness actual remains missing. Later ministry structure should not be read as a gap in continuing-care service delivery or as zero access.

Raw full source pages 64 and 71 are preserved in `scss-2024-25_continuing_care_source_pages.txt`. No source or living-standards files were changed.
