Description: This measure monitors the timeliness of access to continuing care homes in Alberta,
as indicated by the wait times experienced by clients who move into a continuing care home within
30 days of being assessed.

Rationale: This measure speaks to the ministry’s commitment to provide access to a continuing
care home as soon as possible after being assessed, supporting the delivery of highest quality client
care regardless of where they reside in the province.

Results and variance explanation: The 2024-25 result of 63.0 per cent is higher than the 62.5
per cent reported in 2023-24 and shows an increase in the percentage of clients who moved into a
continuing care home within 30 days of being assessed. The preliminary result for 2024-25 is three
percentage points below target.

Investments have been made in home and community care and the number of available continuing
care home spaces is increasing as additional new spaces are identified and planned for. Together,
these investments will provide additional capacity to enable Albertans who need continuing care
homes the ability to access them in a timelier manner.
