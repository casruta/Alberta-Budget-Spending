## Integration limits and next evidence

These outcomes document **historical predecessor-period observations only**. Local2018–25short fiscal reports do not supply matching recent school completion/exam/PISA/crimeoutcomes. The analysis cannot establish quality-of-life improvement/worsening after2019from these alone. Budget intensity is a resource input; completion,achievement andpolice-reported severity are outcomes with different populations,timing and mechanisms.
