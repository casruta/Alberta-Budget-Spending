## Remaining substantive limits

This is a source-verified selected-outcome assessment, not a systematic census of all welfare indicators. Calendar and fiscal outcome windows differ. Published targets are commitments rather than externally standardized adequacy thresholds. Financial CPI and population proxies do not measure education/health sector prices, student needs or age-adjusted demand. Government reports and administrative definitions can exclude recent arrivals, still-waiting patients or unrecorded experiences. Missing 2025–26 fiscal actuals and newer crime observations are local evidence gaps, not claims that newer official data do not exist. No comparison design establishes budget causality. These limits are already substantially disclosed and do not require weakening the descriptive mixed conclusion further.
