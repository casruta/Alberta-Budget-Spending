## Improvements

- Explained nominal spending, inflation adjustment, the population–price benchmark and the limits of spending per resident.
- Clarified emergency waits as a visit-based 90th percentile, completed-surgery results, period life expectancy and mental-health service contact.
- Explained five-year completion, acceptable-standard results and enrolment weighting without treating them as general literacy measures.
- Distinguished mixed housing support, continuing-care access, median AISH decision time and licensed childcare capacity.
- Clarified the poverty basket, earnings interpretation, crime-rate denominators and separate chart scales.
- Added concise topic syntheses without inventing an expert consensus or assigning government causality.
- Kept the archive's forecasts, historical definitions and unverified policy assumptions separate from current findings.
